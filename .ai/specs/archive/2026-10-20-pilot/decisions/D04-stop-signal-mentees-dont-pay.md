# D04 — Stop signal: mentees do not pay
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover session (quick pass)
- Context and the options weighed: three candidate signals were offered for the first invitations and bookings: mentors do not accept or publish availability; mentees do not pay; bookings come only from people the mentor already knows. Founder A chose "mentees do not pay" (people start a booking but abandon at payment, or only say they would pay).
- Decision and why: if mentees start bookings but do not complete payment, or only say they would pay, the team stops or rethinks D03. No threshold or date was set (Q03). The other two signals were not chosen and stay as untested assumptions (A02, A05).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: until a threshold and a date exist, the signal cannot be read as pass or fail.
