# D14 — Mentor payment in the pilot: 150 PLN per completed request, platform keeps 50 PLN
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: mentors cannot accept without knowing what they earn. The Python mentor wanted no bank details before the first booking; the board's earlier revenue note listed a 15 to 20% commission on sessions (the founders' position, not evidence), against 25% here.
- Decision and why: for each completed request the mentor receives 150 PLN and the platform keeps the remaining 50 PLN before payment processing fees. The mentor is asked for bank details only after the first booking, before being paid.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: "completed" is read as the answer being sent (D09); when after that the mentor is paid is not stated (Q13). Processing fees come out of the platform's 50 PLN as worded.
- Status: active
