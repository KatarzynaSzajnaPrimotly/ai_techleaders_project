# D18 — The pilot coordinator sees metadata only, not question or answer text
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: the coordinator could see metadata only (counts, timestamps, payment and refund status) or also the text of questions and answers. D02 says "trust is the product" and R02 says nothing is published without the mentee's approval; refunds under D09 and the measures under D06 need only timestamps. Origin: agent recommendation, accepted by founder A.
- Decision and why: the coordinator sees counts, timestamps and payment and refund status. The coordinator does not read the text of questions or answers.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: a dispute about whether a mentor really answered cannot be settled by reading the text. If text access is wanted later it has to be written into the terms and the privacy notice (D10) first.
- Status: active
