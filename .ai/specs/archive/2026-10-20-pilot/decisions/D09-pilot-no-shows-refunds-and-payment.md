# D09 — Pilot no-show, refund and payment rules
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: the freelancer wanted a same-day refund when the mentor does not come; the Python mentor wanted to keep the payment when the mentee does not show (interviews).
- Decision and why: if the mentor does not provide the answer within the response window (D11), the mentee gets a full refund, initiated within five business days. If the mentee does not reply to a request for more information, the mentor answers from the information already provided. The payment is kept once the answer is sent.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: a refund initiated within five business days is slower than the same-day refund the freelancer asked for (A07). The rule does not say what happens if the mentee is dissatisfied with a delivered answer.
