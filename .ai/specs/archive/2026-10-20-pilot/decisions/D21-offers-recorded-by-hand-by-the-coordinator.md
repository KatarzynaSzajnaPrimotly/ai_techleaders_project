# D21 — The coordinator records offers by hand
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: offers could be recorded by the coordinator by hand, or by the product when a mentee opens a link and gives an e-mail address. Origin: agent recommendation, accepted by founder A, because D07 and D15 count an offer as a concrete offer with a price and a link, which includes people who never pay or never give an e-mail.
- Decision and why: the link is sent personally to each mentee, by the coordinator or by a mentor, and the coordinator records the e-mail address and the date of each offer by hand. That record is the count of offers and the base for cases (D07, D15).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: manual work for the coordinator; mentees who reach the link on their own do not appear as offers, which loses part of the data on how people arrive (A05). How the record reaches the pilot summary is open (Q21).
- Status: active
