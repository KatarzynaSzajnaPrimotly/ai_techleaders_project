# D05 — Now: the unit is a question with a reply window, not a timed session
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: D01 set sessions at 25 or 50 minutes. Three units were offered for the first build: a timed text session (25 or 50 minutes), a question answered within a reply window, or both as two offers. Mentee interviews describe a need for an answer within the hour to the same day; one mentor said 25 minutes is fair but most questions take about 10.
- Decision and why: for Now the mentee pays for a question that the mentor answers in text within a reply window. The length of the window was set later by D11. Sessions of 25 or 50 minutes (D01, R01) stay as the rule for sessions in Later.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: R01 no longer applies to Now; R03 is the replacement (D11 set the window length).
- Status: active; supersedes D01 for Now only
