# D08 — Pilot price: one fixed price of PLN 149 per written answer, set by the platform
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: the workshop left "pricing per mentor or per platform?" open and non-blocking. Mentors spoke of a "high" price or one slightly below others; mentees spoke of 50 to 100 EUR or "below 200 zł" (interviews, not decisions). The staff engineer wanted a price she sets herself.
- Decision and why: for the pilot the platform sets one fixed price of PLN 149 for one written answer, unchanged during the test so results can be compared. It is a test price, not a decision about long-term pricing.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the pilot tests willingness to pay at one price only. Mentors who want to set their own price may decline (A02). How the mentor and the platform share the PLN 149 is not decided (Q11).
- Status: superseded by D13 (2026-10-20, founder A changed the price to PLN 200)
