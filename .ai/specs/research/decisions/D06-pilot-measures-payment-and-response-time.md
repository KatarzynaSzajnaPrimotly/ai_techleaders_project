# D06 — The pilot measures payment and the time to the first answer
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: a pilot that checks only payment would not show whether answers arrive in the time mentees need (A04). The alternative was to limit the test group to people who accept a longer wait, which is easier but does not test the need for a fast answer. Origin: agent recommendation, accepted by founder A.
- Decision and why: the pilot records completed payments and the minutes from payment to the first answer. It needs mentors who commit to the window in D11 (A02).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: no threshold is set for response time; D11 decides what counts as late.
- Status: active
