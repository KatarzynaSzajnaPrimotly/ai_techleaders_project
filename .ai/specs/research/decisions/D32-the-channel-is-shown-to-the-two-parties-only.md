# D32 — The session channel is shown only to the two parties of a confirmed booking
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: the mentor names a channel for the text session (D27). It could be shown on the public mentor page or only to the mentor and mentee of a confirmed booking. Origin: agent recommendation, accepted by founder A, because a public address lets a mentee agree a session with the mentor outside DevMentor and skip the payment.
- Decision and why: the channel address is shown only to the mentor and the mentee of a confirmed booking; the public mentor page does not show it.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: once a booking is confirmed either party can still move the session outside DevMentor; the product cannot prevent that.
- Status: active
