# D31 — One role per account
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: an account could hold one role (mentee, mentor or operator) or several at once. Origin: agent recommendation, accepted by founder A, because `Session.role` stays a single string and the protected session surface does not change.
- Decision and why: each account has exactly one role. A founder who also mentors uses a separate mentor account.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: a person who is both operator and mentor needs two accounts and two sign-ins; revisit if that becomes a burden.
- Status: active
