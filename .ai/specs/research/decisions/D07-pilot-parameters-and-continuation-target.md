# D07 — Pilot parameters, case definition and continuation target
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: D04 named the stop signal ("mentees do not pay") without a count, period or definition of a case. Founder A chose to set them now.
- Decision and why: run the pilot for four weeks and offer the paid service to up to 10 mentees. Continuation target: at least three unique mentees complete payment within that period. If fewer than three pay, pause and review the offer before deciding whether to continue. A case is a unique mentee who receives a clear offer, price and payment link and does not pay within 24 hours; this includes someone who starts a booking and does not pay, or says they would pay but does not book. General statements made before a concrete offer do not count. Founder A coordinates the pilot and makes the go/no-go decision; the mentor provides the paid answers. The target is a provisional choice by founder A; no baseline or other material supports the number three.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the start date is not set; what happens if fewer than 10 mentees receive an offer is open (Q09, closed by D12). Read the result when the four weeks end.
- Status: active (case definition and coordinator); the continuation rule is superseded by D12, then D15 where they conflict. Supersedes D04
