# D23 — The e-mail thread passes through a platform address (relay)
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: with the answer in an e-mail thread (D20) the platform does not see when the mentor answered. Three ways to learn it: a relay through a platform address, the mentor marking the request answered in the product, or the coordinator recording it by hand. Origin: agent recommendation, accepted by founder A, because D06 and D09 need the time of the answer without a human step.
- Decision and why: mentor and mentee write to each other through an address of the platform, which relays the messages and records their times. The relay hides the addresses as described in the question; D16 says the mentor sees the mentee's e-mail, so until Q22 is answered the mentor sees it and the mentee does not see the mentor's address.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the most build of the three options, against a 25-hour limit that is not estimated; message text passes through the platform, so the privacy notice (D10) has to cover it; the coordinator still does not read text (D18). Whether the relay hides the mentee's address from the mentor and whether the platform keeps the text are open (Q22); how the platform tells a request for more information from the answer is open (Q23).
