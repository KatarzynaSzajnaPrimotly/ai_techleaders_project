# D30 — The amount owed to a mentor is the price less the 20% fee
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the om-discover refresh
- Context and the options weighed: D28 has the operator pay mentors by hand in the first iteration while the automatic Connect split stays in 1.1, but a payout needs an amount. The agent drafted it as the price less the 20% fee of D11; the alternative was the full price with the fee settled outside the product.
- Decision and why: the amount owed to a mentor for a paid session is the session price less the 20% platform fee (D11). The first iteration records it for every paid session so the operator can pay by hand and log it (D28, R18). The automatic Connect split and payout stay in 1.1 (E03-S06).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: what a late-cancelled, forfeited booking owes the mentor is not decided (open for E03-S05 and E03-S06).
